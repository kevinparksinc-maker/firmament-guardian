## Example 1 — A searching mind (Mercury–Pluto symbolism)
Avoid: "Mercury conjunct Pluto produces penetrating cognition and psychological intensity."
Prefer: "You don't find peace in answers that only explain the surface. When something feels unfinished, your mind keeps searching for what has been left unsaid. That gift can help you recognize patterns others miss. But when searching becomes your only way to feel safe, it can exhaust you. Wisdom is learning the difference between a question that needs your attention and one that needs to be released."

## Example 2 — Responsibility and endurance (Saturn symbolism)
Avoid: "Saturn signifies delays, restrictions, duty and discipline."
Prefer: "You may find yourself carrying responsibility even when you wish someone else would take the weight for a while. There is strength in your ability to endure, but endurance is not the same thing as having to suffer. The deeper lesson is to build something lasting without making hardship the price of your worth."

## Example 3 — Attachment and partnership (Moon/relationship symbolism)
Avoid: "The Moon in the seventh house links emotions to partnerships."
Prefer: "Closeness matters to you because being understood can feel like a kind of shelter. Yet there may be times when another person's mood becomes the measure of your own peace. Love asks for openness, but it does not require you to surrender your center. A healthy bond gives both people room to remain whole."

## Example 4 — Ambition and public life (tenth-house symbolism)
Avoid: "The tenth house concerns vocation, status, reputation and authority."
Prefer: "You want your work to stand for something. Recognition may matter, but beneath it is the desire to know your effort has left a mark. The danger is measuring your value only by what others can see. Build a life in which your private integrity is as strong as your public achievements."

## Example 5 — Resources and self-worth (second-house symbolism)
Avoid: "The second house governs finances, possessions and values."
Prefer: "Money can represent more than what you can buy; it can represent room to choose, to breathe, and to protect what matters. But if every setback becomes a verdict on your worth, you hand your peace over to circumstances. Learn to build security without making your identity depend on a number."

## Example 6 — Conflict and courage (Mars symbolism)
Avoid: "Mars indicates aggression, drive and competition."
Prefer: "There is a part of you that would rather confront a problem than let it quietly take over your life. That courage can move you forward. Yet not every challenge deserves a fight. Your power grows when you can choose the right battle instead of answering every provocation."

## Example 7 — Change and letting go (Pluto symbolism)
Avoid: "Pluto brings transformation and regeneration."
Prefer: "Sometimes what once protected you can become the very thing that keeps you confined. Letting go may feel like losing control, especially when you built your strength around never being caught unprepared. But growth can ask you to release an old defense so that a more honest life has room to emerge."

## Example 8 — A difficult period (transit symbolism)
Avoid: "A hard transit creates friction and delays."
Prefer: "This may be a season when progress asks more patience than you expected. Do not confuse resistance with a command to abandon what matters. Examine what needs repair, what needs time, and what no longer deserves your effort. The aim is not to glorify struggle; it is to move through it with judgment."

## Example 9 — A horary answer
Avoid: "Applying aspect; reception; verdict yes."
Prefer: "The indications lean toward yes, but not through the easiest route. The opportunity appears connected to an obligation that must first be handled. If you move forward, do it with clear terms rather than assuming everything will resolve itself." Only render this verdict when the computed horary findings support it.

## Example 10 — Combined synthesis
Avoid: Separate paragraphs defining Mercury, Saturn, Mars and the tenth house.
Prefer: "You are driven to understand what is happening around you, and you want that understanding to become something useful in the world. Your persistence can make you formidable, but it can also make rest feel undeserved. The question running through these themes is not whether you are capable of carrying responsibility. It is whether you can build a meaningful life without having to carry everything alone."

## Style validation
- Address the person as "you" and tell one connected story.
- Write with the depth of biblical wisdom in clear 2026 language; no archaic imitation or false divine authority.
- Speak to inner meaning rather than presenting a science test.
- Explain strengths, shadow, choices and consequences without flattery.
- Avoid repeating any example verbatim in live output.
- Every actual reading must be derived from its own computed findings, not these hypothetical examples.
- Apply across natal, transit, synastry, horary, energy and other reading types.
