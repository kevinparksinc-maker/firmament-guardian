# Bible Believers Astrology design brief

## Direction
**Astral Observatory / Quiet Guardian** — a calm, dark-sky interface for reflective chart work, built around a restrained observatory palette and precise data surfaces.

## Design dimensions
- **Movement:** Modern editorial interface with astronomical instrument cues.
- **Core principles:** Clarity before mystique; data and interpretation stay legible; emotional warmth without claiming certainty.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep midnight and slate foundations with cyan and violet accents for celestial contrast.
- **Layout paradigm:** Spacious single-column reading workspace with responsive cards, clear sections, and focused action areas.
- **Signature elements:** Fine constellation lines, chart rings, small uppercase labels, soft borders, and a steady guide/guardian voice.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Make complex chart work feel approachable; keep controls explicit and pair voice controls with equivalent text controls.
- **Animation:** Purposeful, subtle transitions only; preserve reading focus.
- **Typography system:** Serif display accents for the observatory/editorial voice paired with a clean sans-serif UI face.
- **Brand essence:** A thoughtful guide for navigating symbolic sky language.
- **Brand voice:** Supportive, precise, grounded, and transparent about uncertainty.
- **Wordmark/logo:** A compact three-point guardian star inside a circular orbit, rendered as a bold flat silhouette.
- **Signature brand color:** Observatory cyan `#67e8f9` against midnight navy `#08111f`.
